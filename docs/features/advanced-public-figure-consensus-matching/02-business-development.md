# Business Development

Twinzy remains free by default. This feature adds no subscription, account, payment, advertising, endorsement, or commercial relationship with a public figure.

The value is retention and trust: more explainable regional/global recall, verified informational links, and licensed image attribution. Rollout is global but flag-gated and benchmark-led. Support/help copy must state that public images and biographies are informational and never used for matching.

There is no SLA or contractual commitment to a particular candidate, image, biography, uptime of external sources, or accuracy percentage.
