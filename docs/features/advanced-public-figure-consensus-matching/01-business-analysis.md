# Business Analysis

## Problem

Current open-ended model recall can overvalue styling signals, miss regionally familiar figures, produce unverifiable names, and expose inconsistent localized reasoning. Result cards also combine Arabic and Latin dynamic text in a fragile directional node.

## Stakeholders and desired state

Players receive familiar, explainable entertainment results; support can trace safe failure classes; engineering can benchmark recall without personal photos; rights holders receive source/license attribution. Results remain free-by-default and privacy-safe.

## Goals and measures

Improve expected-entity recall@25/top-10/top-5, regional and structure-first recall, stable-evidence precision, entity-resolution accuracy, licensed-image coverage, and wrong-language rate. Measure latency/cost and unsupported-claim/contradiction rates. No numeric accuracy claim ships without labeled evidence.

## Assumptions and dependencies

Provider credentials, model routes, catalog data, and Wikimedia availability are deployment concerns. All flags remain off until benchmark and owner acceptance. The existing analysis endpoint and no-account model remain unchanged.

## Risk of no change

Unfamiliar or accessory-dominated results, unverifiable biographies/images, mixed-language Arabic output, accessibility defects, and inability to measure accuracy consistently.
